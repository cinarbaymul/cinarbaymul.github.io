Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Family Assistant ("the app") is a private family assistant that helps manage family calendar events and reminders. It is operated by Cinar Baymul. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how that data is used, and how access can be removed.

Data the app accesses

Family Assistant accesses Google Calendar data through Google's OAuth authorisation. It uses this access only to read, create, update, and manage the family calendar. This includes event details such as titles, dates and times, descriptions, locations, participants, and reminder settings on that calendar.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used solely to provide the functionality the user requests — for example, showing upcoming events, adding a new event, rescheduling an existing one, or setting a reminder. It is not used for any other purpose.

What the app does not do

  • Your data is not sold to anyone.
  • Your data is not shared with advertisers and is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.
  • Your data is not used to train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Your data is not shared with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens issued by Google are stored securely and are used only to make the calendar requests described above. Access is limited to the operator of the app, and calendar data is not retained beyond what is needed to carry out the requested action.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google data at any time from your Google Account, under Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services, or directly at myaccount.google.com/connections. Once access is revoked, the stored OAuth token no longer works and the app can no longer reach your calendar.

Limited Use disclosure

Family Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or about the data the app handles, contact cinarbaymul@gmail.com.
