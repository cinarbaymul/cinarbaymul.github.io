Family Assistant

Family Assistant is a private family assistant that helps manage family calendar events and reminders. It is operated by Cinar Baymul.

What it does

Family Assistant is used by one household to keep a shared family calendar organised. On request, it can:

  • Read upcoming events on the family calendar
  • Create new events
  • Update or reschedule existing events
  • Set and manage reminders for those events

Google Calendar access

Family Assistant connects to Google Calendar through Google's official OAuth sign-in. It uses that access only to read, create, update, and manage the family calendar — nothing else. Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Privacy: Family Assistant does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
